K-means on a single 1D dataset

If you had only x1 values: [15, 19, 15, 5, 13, 17, 15, 12, 8, 6, 9, 13]

Pick 2 initial centroids: c1=13, c2=9
Distance calculation becomes simpler (just 1D): |15-13|=2, |15-9|=6
Assign each point to nearest centroid
Compute mean of each cluster
Repeat

That's it—no second feature needed.
